# Skiggo Product Specification

## Product
Skiggo is a multi-category, location-aware service marketplace for Nigeria first, with international expansion as a later goal. Tagline: **Get It Done.**

## Primary users
- Visitors discovering services and providers
- Customers booking services or requesting quotations
- Providers managing listings, availability, quotations and jobs
- Least-privilege platform administrators

## Core marketplace models
1. Fixed-price booking
2. Customer quote request and provider quotations
3. Project-based hiring with milestones and approved scope changes

## Trust and correctness
Never fabricate providers, ratings, bookings, payments, verification, notifications or marketplace metrics. Reviews require eligible completed bookings. Payment success must be verified server-side. Admin permissions must be enforced server-side and at the database layer.

## Initial categories
Home repairs; cleaning and domestic services; beauty and personal care; automotive; moving, delivery and errands; education and tutoring; digital and creative services; business services; events; fashion and tailoring; commercial and industrial services; agriculture and specialist services.

## Delivery strategy
Implement foundation and public discovery first, then authentication and data security, catalogue, matching, bookings, quotations, communication, financial operations, verification and administration. Advanced AI and live tracking follow only after core workflows are reliable.
